Disease grouping — for approval

Two levels. Diagnoses is every raw value CattleMax recorded and the group it maps to. Disease groups is what those groups then produce clinically — case counts, case fatality, relapse, and when in the phase they strike. Rename or merge a group by typing a new name; nothing is applied until you hand the block at the bottom back.

herd pulled cases episode gap days no diagnosis recorded events
Nothing is ever dropped. A therapeutic treatment with no diagnosis recorded still counts as a case — it appears as Unknown disease so it stays visible in every rate. Type a new name in rename / merge into to combine groups; give two groups the same name and they merge.

Case fatality is deaths within the death window after the case ended, over cases. Median day is where in the phase the disease typically strikes — a low number means it hits early, which is why a flat per-day rate misleads.
