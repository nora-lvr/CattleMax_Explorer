Intent is derived, not stored. Vaccines, antiparasitics and insecticides count as routine work; antibiotics and anti-inflammatories count as therapeutic; and any product given to an animal that already has a recorded diagnosis is promoted to therapeutic. That last rule is why some drugs show two intents. Change the drug class and the intent follows.
