Trend across cohorts

Each point is a group that entered the phase together. Spring and Fall are separate lines and the axis is the year, so a season that runs worse every year shows as a standing gap rather than a saw-tooth. One panel per disease, each on its own scale — the patterns differ far more than the levels do. Hollow points are groups with 10% or more of the animals still in the phase at the pull date, so those figures can still rise.

The value plotted is the attack rate: the share of animals entering that phase in that group treated at least once for that disease. It counts every animal that entered, including those still in the phase or that left part way through, so it is a floor. A case is a run of therapeutic treatments on one animal for one disease — treatments closer together than the episode gap are the same case. Treatments with no diagnosis recorded are reported as Unknown disease rather than dropped, so they still count against every rate. Every record excluded anywhere in the pipeline is listed in the exclusions ledger that ships beside the data.
