Which cows deliver a calf to transition

A calf has succeeded when it becomes a product or a producer — sold, calved, or put to work as a sire. What happens to that animal afterwards is its own record, not its dam's, so a heifer who calved and later died still counts as a calf delivered.

The outcome ladder

Every calving in the record, reported at its exact current status. An outcome is final when it can no longer change, and preliminary when the calf is alive but has not yet been sold or calved. Preliminary seasons are carried, never counted as failures — a calf still growing is not a calf lost.

The three ways a calf is lost

This split decides what you do next. A calf dead in the first week is a calving and dam problem — that follows the cow. A calf dead before weaning is scours and pneumonia. A calf dead after weaning is a growing-cattle problem. Only the first is reliably a cull conversation.
